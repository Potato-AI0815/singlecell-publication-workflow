# Research-use disclaimer

This software is provided for research and educational use. It is not a medical device, clinical decision-support system, diagnostic test, treatment recommendation engine or substitute for professional statistical and bioinformatics review.

Outputs can be wrong because of data quality, metadata errors, inappropriate experimental design, package defects, model assumptions, reference mismatch or user configuration. Users are responsible for verifying sample identities, statistical units, annotations, methods, figures and biological interpretations.

Expression-inferred CNA, cell–cell communication, drug-response scores, trajectories and virtual perturbations are model-derived exploratory outputs. They require independent evidence and must not be presented as direct causal, genomic, pharmacological or clinical findings without appropriate validation.
